What it does

⛽

Station balance

Advances, purchases and the remaining balance for each station. If you owe money it shows as a negative balance, and it warns you when the balance runs low.

🚚

Drivers and team

Drivers enter only their own purchases. Managers see everything, and every change is recorded: who changed what, and when.

🧾

Receipt photos

Attach the receipt or payment slip to the entry. Everyone in the team sees the same photo, so the proof stays even if the paper is lost.

📊

Monthly report

Monthly fuel cost, litres and average price per vehicle, driver or station. Excel and PDF for your accountant.

💬

Send on WhatsApp

Send the statement and report to your accountant, partner or the station with one tap.

☁️

Cloud backup

Your records are safe even if the phone is lost. Several phones can share one ledger, protected with a PIN lock.

Install on your phone

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open hisapo.com/app.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open hisapo.com/app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon at the bottom.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
