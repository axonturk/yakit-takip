Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Hisapo is an app for keeping track of prepaid fuel advances and credit accounts at fuel stations. This page explains what information is kept, where, and what it is used for.

1. Information that stays on your phone

If you do not turn on cloud backup, your stations, purchases, advances, vehicle numbers, notes and receipt photos are stored only on your own phone (in the browser's storage) and are never sent to us. Your app lock PIN is kept on the phone only as a summary that cannot be reversed. Contacts you save for sending on WhatsApp also stay only on that phone.

2. Cloud backup and shared ledger

If you turn on cloud backup, you sign in with your email address. Your email address, the records in your ledger, receipt photos and the history of changes to those records (who changed what, and when) are then stored on the servers of our service provider, Supabase. Only people who joined that ledger with its invite code can see this information. The ledger's creator can change the roles of team members or remove them.

3. Anonymous usage and error information

To improve the app, one anonymous "opened" record per device per day (a random device number, app version, record counts, theme) and any error messages are sent. These records never contain amounts, station names, vehicle numbers, notes, email addresses or photos.

4. What your information is not used for

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising or share it with third parties. The app has no ads and no ad tracking.

5. Deletion and your rights

You can remove the records on your phone by deleting the app or the browser data. To ask for your cloud account and records to be deleted, or to use your other rights under data protection law, write to us.
